# SearchApi wordmark

`search-api-light.svg` and `search-api-dark.svg` are SearchApi's wordmark, used here to credit the Jobs and News data provider. The files come from the [Business Directory Toolkit's SearchApi logo assets](https://github.com/pooyagolchian/business-directory-toolkit/tree/main/logo). The mark remains SearchApi's trademark; this project's MIT licence applies to the app code.
