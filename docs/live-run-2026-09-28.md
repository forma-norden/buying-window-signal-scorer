The exact lens is in [`examples/case-study-settings.json`](../examples/case-study-settings.json).

This is a single bounded sample, **not a market estimate**. The 12 employers are selected from the returned listings. Google Jobs' coverage, our search phrases, the one-page-per-query limit and the 12-account ceiling all affect the result.

## Three stronger hiring signals

Each scored 60/100 in this scan: 40 role points, 20 recency points, and no news or convergence points. Their job descriptions show *different* possible buying contexts, which a tie in the score cannot resolve:

1. **[Safe Software](https://www.safe.com/careers/)** listed a VP, Revenue Operations role. Its description said the CRM was not fully configured and the AI tooling stack was unevaluated. The official careers page also listed the role when checked on 28 September.
2. **[Matia](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/matia/1b579e8a-0eb6-4c15-90d3-851786caac65)** described its Revenue Operations opening as its first dedicated RevOps hire and a mandate to build the GTM foundation.
3. **[NetBox Labs](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/netboxlabs/6db20463-5f41-4aaa-8118-081c76e4f01f)** sought a Senior Sales Operations Manager to work on pipeline, forecasting and GTM systems architecture.

The job title, description and age came through Google Jobs. The linked employer-controlled careers or ATS pages were checked separately to confirm the roles; their current state may change. The score does not assert that any of these organisations are purchasing software.

## The false match that changed the code

The first implementation gave **NICE** 100/100 by pairing a recent job attributed to NICE with Google News stories about the unrelated UK National Institute for Health and Care Excellence. A same-name string match was insufficient. We changed the rule: news for a short, ambiguous company name or a name found only in a snippet remains visible but earns no news or convergence points. NICE now receives **45/100** from its dated job title alone; its identity remains low-confidence. The regression test fixes this behaviour.

Likely intermediaries, including names resembling job boards, also appeared in discovery. They are flagged rather than quietly removed because the API's employer field does not establish the ultimate hiring company. Users must confirm the employer and ICP before outreach.

## Reproduction and data handling

Install the repository, copy the case-study lens to ignored `data/config.json`, add a SearchApi key to ignored `.env`, and run `python -m buying_window.cli scan --mode discovery --max-accounts 12`. The local archive stores raw responses with credentials redacted; it is intentionally excluded from the public repository. The bundled fictional demo fixture can be replayed without an API key. A rerun will produce different results because jobs and news are live.
